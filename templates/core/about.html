About SignLearn

SignLearn is a platform for learning Indian Sign Language (ISL) — starting with the alphabet, and growing into full sign recognition practice and testing powered by computer vision.

01

Learn

Go through the ISL alphabet one letter at a time, with images and descriptions for each sign.

02

Practice

Use your webcam to practice signs in real time. Our recognition model checks your sign and awards points as you go.

03

Test

Take structured tests to measure how well you know the alphabet, with a full breakdown of your results.
